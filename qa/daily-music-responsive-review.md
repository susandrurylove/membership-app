# Daily Teaching and Healing Music Responsive Review

## Desktop dashboard and reminder popup

The isolated MySQL-backed preview rendered the production member shell, Daily Teaching promotion, and four primary journey cards correctly. The Daily Teaching card showed the deterministic day number, concise title and summary, topic image, reading action, and reminder-management action. The accessible modal opened automatically for a member with no saved preference and presented two clear options—Daily and Weekly—plus a non-destructive Not now action. Text contrast, button hierarchy, image treatment, and the SusanDrury.com navy/gold/ivory/teal visual system remained consistent.

The Healing Music dashboard card appeared as the third journey destination using the approved listening artwork, with the card grid adapting to the additional item without crowding the shell. No horizontal overflow was visible in the desktop review.

## Preference save and Daily Teaching reading flow

Choosing **Daily** in the popup saved successfully to the isolated MySQL database, closed the dialog, displayed a success confirmation, and changed the card action to “Daily reminders.” The selected timezone and 8:00 delivery preference were accepted through the protected API.

The Daily Teaching reading route opened correctly with a topic-specific image, 225-word body, visible source attribution and canonical SusanDrury.com link, reflection prompt, practice invitation, and educational safety note. The editorial layout remained compact and scannable with the main reading surface and supporting cards arranged clearly at desktop width.

## Healing Music library and playback

The dedicated music route rendered all **23 tracks** with SusanDrury.com-aligned navy, gold, ivory, and teal styling. The hero, central now-playing surface, duration labels, body-system chips, and two-column desktop card layout were clear and restrained. Selecting **Beautiful Instrument** updated the now-playing title, selected-state card, and audio source, and browser playback entered the playing state against the Susan website Bunny MP3. The page exposes only one active audio player, avoiding overlapping playback and reducing mobile complexity.

## Four-viewport visual review

The Healing Music library remains readable and touch-friendly from 320 px through 1440 px: titles wrap naturally, duration remains visible, body-system chips do not overflow, and every Listen control uses a full-width mobile hit area. The mobile music screenshots begin at the selected track because the verification click scrolled that control into view; this is test position rather than a layout defect.

The dashboard has no horizontal overflow and the four journey cards balance correctly at tablet and desktop widths. The review did identify one flow improvement: at 320 px and 430 px, the tall welcome hero pushes the new Daily Teaching promotion below the initial viewport. The final layout will therefore place the Daily Teaching card before the welcome hero on phones while retaining the welcome-first hierarchy from tablet upward. Remote image placeholders visible in automated captures are a capture-timing artifact; direct browser review confirmed the same Bunny images render.

## Final narrow-mobile review

At 320 px, the Daily Teaching promotion now begins immediately below the member header, so the day number, teaching title, and topic imagery are discoverable without first traversing the welcome hero. The bottom navigation remains clear and does not cover the primary heading. The Daily Teaching detail uses a single-column ceremonial hero with readable title wrapping, generous side padding, and correct bottom-navigation clearance; supporting reflection, practice, safety, and source sections follow naturally below the fold.

The browser matrix passed all 12 focused cases across 320 px, 430 px, 768 px, and 1440 px after the contrast correction, including zero serious or critical WCAG 2 A/AA violations on the Healing Music library and no horizontal overflow on any tested surface.
