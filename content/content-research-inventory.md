## Editorial and Safety Position

The supplied and official material will be presented as **Susan Drury’s spiritual, reflective, and educational teachings**, not as medical diagnosis or a substitute for professional medical care. Body-system associations should be framed as invitations for self-inquiry rather than causal medical claims. Every body-focused teaching template will include a calm notice encouraging members to seek qualified medical support for symptoms, urgent concerns, or treatment decisions.
